Cloud Migration

Why the Cloud

Stop depending on one server in one office

Many businesses still run key software on a server in the office. When that machine fails, the power goes, or someone needs to work from home, the business slows down. Upgrades are delayed, backups are uncertain, and growth means buying more hardware.

Cloud migration moves those applications, files and databases to a cloud platform, so your team can work securely from anywhere, backups run automatically, and capacity grows with you. We plan the move around your business, not the other way round.

If your data needs cleaning or restructuring first, we combine the move with database modernisation.

What you gain

  • Access your systems securely from anywhere
  • Automatic backups and faster recovery
  • Capacity that grows without buying new hardware
  • Fewer surprises from ageing office servers
  • A planned move with a fallback at every step

FAQ

Cloud Migration: common questions

What is cloud migration?

Cloud migration is moving your applications, databases and files from servers you own, usually in your office, to a cloud platform run by a provider, so they are accessible, backed up and scalable.

Which cloud platforms do you work with?

We work with major platforms such as Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, and with cloud business apps like Zoho. We recommend a platform based on your applications, team and budget.

Will there be downtime during the move?

We plan to keep downtime as short as possible, usually by testing everything in the cloud first and switching over at a time that suits you, with a fallback if anything goes wrong.

Is our data safe in the cloud?

Cloud platforms offer strong security, but it has to be set up properly. We configure user access, roles, encryption options and backups as part of the move.

How much does cloud migration cost?

It depends on how many systems, how much data and how many users are involved. After a first consultation we share a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost before any work starts. Cloud hosting fees are billed by the cloud provider separately.

Do we have to move everything at once?

No. Many businesses move in stages, starting with email and files or one application, then moving the rest once the team is comfortable.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Our office is in Mundhwa, Pune, so we can meet your team in person. We also work remotely with businesses across India.

Ready to move off the office server?

Tell us what you run today. We’ll assess it and suggest a step-by-step migration plan with a written scope.
