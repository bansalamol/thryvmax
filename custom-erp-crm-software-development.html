What is the difference between custom ERP and off-the-shelf ERP?

Off-the-shelf ERP gives you standard modules you configure; custom ERP is built around the way your business already works. Custom takes more effort upfront but avoids paying for features you don’t use and forcing your team into workarounds. We compare both in our custom vs off-the-shelf ERP guide.

Can you build a CRM and an ERP that work as one system?

Yes. We can build CRM and ERP as one connected system, so a lead that becomes a customer flows straight into orders, invoicing and reporting without anyone re-entering data.

Can you migrate data from our spreadsheets or current software?

Yes. We map your existing data, clean it, and move it into the new system before go-live, then check it with your team so nothing is lost.

How much does custom ERP or CRM development cost?

It depends on scope: the number of users, modules, integrations and how much data needs to move. After a first consultation we share a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost, so you know what you are paying for before any work starts.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We start by understanding how your business runs today, design the right solution with you, then develop, deploy and deliver it. After launch we train your team and stay on for support and improvements.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Our office is in Mundhwa, Pune, so teams across Pune can meet us in person. We also work remotely with businesses across India.
