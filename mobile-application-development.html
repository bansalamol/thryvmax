FAQ

Mobile Application Development: common questions

Still unsure? Ask us directly or call +91 97080 81818 or +91 90962 50998.

Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build apps for Android and iOS and recommend the right approach, native or cross-platform, based on your features, audience and budget.

Can the app connect to our ERP, CRM or website?

Yes. We connect apps to your existing systems, including Zoho, Odoo and custom ERP or CRM, so data stays in sync.

Do you help publish the app on the Play Store and App Store?

Yes. We prepare the app for release and help you through the store submission process.

How much does mobile app development cost?

It depends on scope: the number of users, modules, integrations and how much data needs to move. After a first consultation we share a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost, so you know what you are paying for before any work starts.

Do you maintain the app after launch?

Yes. We start by understanding how your business runs today, design the right solution with you, then develop, deploy and deliver it. After launch we train your team and stay on for support and improvements.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Our office is in Mundhwa, Pune, so teams across Pune can meet us in person. We also work remotely with businesses across India.
