Business Automation Solutions: common questions

Still unsure? Ask us directly or call +91 97080 81818 or +91 90962 50998.

Which business processes can be automated?

Most rule-based, repetitive work: data entry between systems, approvals, reminders and follow-ups, report generation, invoice creation and notifications by email, SMS or WhatsApp.

Do we need to replace our current software to automate?

Usually not. We often automate around the tools you already use, such as Zoho, Odoo or custom systems, by connecting them and adding workflows.

How do you decide what to automate first?

We map your current process with your team, then start with the tasks that take the most time or cause the most errors, so you see value early.

How much does business automation cost?

It depends on scope: the number of users, modules, integrations and how much data needs to move. After a first consultation we share a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost, so you know what you are paying for before any work starts.

What happens if a process changes later?

Workflows can be updated as your business changes. We document what we build and offer ongoing support so automation keeps up with you.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Our office is in Mundhwa, Pune, so teams across Pune can meet us in person. We also work remotely with businesses across India.
