What can be customised in Odoo?

Almost everything: fields and forms, workflows and approvals, reports and print formats, dashboards, access rights, and new modules built for your process. We also connect Odoo to other systems.

Will customisation cause problems when we upgrade Odoo?

We build customisations as separate modules rather than editing Odoo’s core code, which keeps upgrades far more manageable.

Is Odoo or Zoho better for my business?

It depends on your processes, team and budget. Odoo is strong for inventory and manufacturing; Zoho is strong for sales, CRM and a wide set of ready apps. We compare them in Zoho vs Odoo for small businesses.

How much does Odoo customisation cost?

It depends on scope: the number of users, modules, integrations and how much data needs to move. After a first consultation we share a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost, so you know what you are paying for before any work starts.

Can you take over an Odoo system someone else built?

Yes. We review the existing setup and custom code first, then recommend fixes and improvements before building anything new.

Our office is in Mundhwa, Pune, so teams across Pune can meet us in person. We also work remotely with businesses across India.
