Do you work with Odoo Community or Odoo Enterprise?

We work with both. Community is the free, open-source edition; Enterprise is Odoo’s paid edition with extra apps and support. We help you choose based on the features you need.

Will custom modules break when we upgrade Odoo?

We build customisations as separate modules rather than changing Odoo’s core code. Upgrades still need testing, but this keeps them far more manageable.
