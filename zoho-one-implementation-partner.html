What does a Zoho implementation partner do?

A partner plans your Zoho setup, configures the apps, migrates your data, connects the tools you use, and trains your team, so you get a working system instead of a set of unused apps. Read why the right partner matters.

Should we choose Zoho One or individual Zoho apps?

Zoho One suits businesses that will use several Zoho apps across departments. If you only need CRM or Books, individual apps can be simpler. We recommend based on what your team actually needs.

Can you migrate our data and email to Zoho?

Yes. We migrate customer and business data from spreadsheets or other systems, and email to Zoho Mail, then verify it with your team before go-live.

How much does Zoho implementation cost?

It depends on scope: the number of users, modules, integrations and how much data needs to move. After a first consultation we share a written proposal with scope, timeline and cost, so you know what you are paying for before any work starts. Zoho licence fees are paid to Zoho separately.

What happens after go-live?

We train your team, stay on for support, and keep improving the setup as your business grows.

Do you work with businesses outside Pune?

Our office is in Mundhwa, Pune, so teams across Pune can meet us in person. We also work remotely with businesses across India.
